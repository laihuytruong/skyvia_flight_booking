# Prompt tạo bộ ảnh giao diện

Bốn board được tạo bằng công cụ sinh ảnh tích hợp. Mỗi board là một ảnh gồm tám màn hình; dùng SCREEN-CATALOG.md để triển khai chính xác các trường và trạng thái.

## Board 1

Use case: ui-mockup. Create one polished high resolution landscape UI presentation board for Skyvia, a Vietnamese flight booking platform with AI copilot. Exactly EIGHT complete desktop website screen miniatures arranged in a strict 2 columns x 4 rows grid, generous gutters, each screenshot aspect ratio 16:9, consistent design. Flat screenshot views, no devices or perspective. White and pale blue surfaces, deep navy typography, royal blue primary actions, restrained teal accents, clean Inter-like font, rounded cards, realistic production content. Use short Vietnamese labels and realistic VND prices. Each screen has its large numbered title above it. All eight screens must be distinct and completely visible, no cropped edges. Prioritize coherent UI structure over tiny text. Brand Skyvia visible on each header. Do not include technical frameworks, code, placeholder lorem ipsum, watermarks or presentation decoration. 01 / KHÁM PHÁ & TÀI KHOẢN. Screens: 01 Trang chủ: airplane travel hero, large flight search form HAN to SGN round trip, dates, passengers, AI chat launcher. 02 Kết quả tìm kiếm: filters left, flight cards middle, sort and price calendar. 03 Chi tiết chuyến bay: outbound and return legs, fare comparison Economy Basic/Flex, baggage and cancellation terms. 04 Đăng nhập: email/password, Google button, travel photo panel. 05 Đăng ký: name email password consent form. 06 Xác minh email: six digit OTP input, resend countdown. 07 Quên mật khẩu: email entry and reset email confirmation card. 08 Đặt mật khẩu mới: password and confirm fields, strength indicator, success state.
